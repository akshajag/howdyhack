THE 12TH TIDE  -  play it on your own computer
==============================================

A coastal endless runner in the browser. Run the beach and the pier, jet-ski Bolivar Channel, surf Shorebreak Point,
dive into the coral canyon and the shipwreck grotto (and past the Kraken at the back of it), and ride out the 12th
tide. Play as the runner, or as Reveille (the land course: no diving). Steer with your body, your head or your hand in front of the
webcam, or with the keyboard.

QUICK START (no install beyond Python, which most Macs and Linux machines already have)
  macOS    double-click  start-mac.command   (if it's blocked: right-click it > Open)
  Windows  double-click  start-windows.bat
  Linux    run           ./start-linux.sh
  ...or in a terminal in this folder:   python3 serve.py   (Windows: py serve.py)

A browser tab opens at http://127.0.0.1:8080 . Keep the little server window open while you play; close it (or
Ctrl+C) to stop. If you don't have Python, the scripts fall back to Node.js (npx http-server). Opening
play/index.html directly from the file system does NOT work: browsers block that, so it needs the local server.

BROWSER: a recent Chrome or Edge is best (Firefox and Safari work too). A laptop or desktop with a GPU; it adapts the
graphics quality automatically (Settings lets you choose).

DEMO COURSE: on the title screen, switch "Demo course" on. It's a set course that shows the whole game in about three
minutes (beach, pier, surfing, the reef, the grotto, the storm and the 12th tide, then the rainbow), gentler than a
normal run so a presenter gets through it. Same as the seed DEMO.

CONTROLS
  Keyboard   A / D or arrows: switch lanes (hold to steer under water)   Space or W: jump   S: slide
             W / S under water: swim up / down   Shift: surge   Esc: pause   R: restart
             On the board: the same; from the left lane, A again carves up the wave and launches you off the lip
             (a backflip and a 360; A / D in the air spin more, Space grabs)
  Camera     "Play with the camera", then pick Body, Head or Hand and hold still a second to calibrate.
             Body: lean or step left / right for a lane, hop to jump, duck to slide, both hands up to surge;
                   under water your body is where you swim.
             Head: slip your head left / right (one lane per slip, coming back to the middle does nothing), bob up to
                   jump, duck to slide; under water your head is where you swim.
             Hand: hand left / centre / right picks the lane, flick up = jump, flick down = slide, fist = surge.
             With a camera the obstacles get 30% more room.
  Jet ski   hold A / D to steer anywhere across the channel (camera: you ride where you are), Space hops, S ducks;
            hit a floating ramp for a backflip.
  The first run is a short slow-motion tutorial (it can be replayed from How to play).

The camera tracking models (MediaPipe) load from the internet the first time, so camera play needs a connection;
keyboard play works offline. The camera only works from a local address or https, which is why the game runs on
127.0.0.1.

HANDY URL OPTIONS (add to the address, e.g. http://127.0.0.1:8080/?seed=DEMO&tutorial=0)
  ?seed=ABC123     the same course every time (DEMO: the demo course)   ?tutorial=0   skip the tutorial
  ?quality=Low|Medium|High                                             ?mute=1       no sound

FOR DEVELOPERS: the source is in source/ (TypeScript + three.js + Vite). With Node.js 18+:
  cd source
  npm install
  npm run dev          -> http://127.0.0.1:5173
  npm run build        -> a fresh build in source/dist
README.md describes the game; ASSET_CREDITS.md lists the third-party models (CC0 / CC-BY) and their creators.
